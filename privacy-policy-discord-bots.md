# Miku.N Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Miku.N processes and how it is used.

## 1. Gachapon Messages and Attachments

Miku.N is designed to process messages generated by the Gachapon bot.

When Miku.N detects a relevant Gachapon drop, it may:

* Read the Gachapon bot's message.
* Access the attached card image.
* Temporarily download the image to the bot's server.
* Process the image using locally hosted Tesseract OCR.
* Extract card information from the image.
* Use the extracted information to identify the card and respond with the relevant card data.

By installing Miku.N in a Discord server, the person installing the bot acknowledges that this processing is required for Miku.N's functionality.

## 2. What We Do Not Collect

Miku.N does not intentionally collect or monitor general Discord conversations.

Miku.N does not permanently store:

* General Discord chat messages.
* Gachapon message content.
* Card images after processing.
* OCR-extracted card text as a permanent message record.

## 3. Information That May Be Stored

Miku.N may store information required for its features, including:

* Discord User IDs.
* Discord Server IDs.
* Wishlist information.
* Bot configuration and settings.
* Token or premium-related information, where applicable.

This information is used only to provide and operate Miku.N's features.

## 4. Card Images

Card images are temporarily stored on the bot's server while OCR processing takes place.

The images are deleted after processing by the bot's cleanup process.

Card images are processed using locally hosted Tesseract OCR and are not sent to third-party AI or OCR services.

## 5. AI and Machine Learning

Miku.N does not use Discord messages, card images, or extracted card information to train, fine-tune, or improve AI or machine learning models.

## 6. Data Sharing

Miku.N does not sell user data.

Miku.N does not share Discord message content or card images with third parties for advertising or profiling.

## 7. Removing Miku.N

A server administrator can remove Miku.N from the server at any time.

After Miku.N is removed, it will no longer process future Gachapon messages from that server.
