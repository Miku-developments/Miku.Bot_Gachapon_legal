# Miku.N Terms of Service

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

## 1. Acceptance of Terms

By inviting, installing, or using **Miku.N** in a Discord server, you agree to these Terms of Service.

If you do not agree with these terms, do not install or use Miku.N.

## 2. What Miku.N Does

Miku.N is a companion utility bot for the Gachapon Discord card game.

Miku.N scans messages and attachments generated by the Gachapon bot to identify card drops and provide useful card information.

Miku.N is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the Gachapon bot or its developers.

## 3. Gachapon Message and Attachment Processing

Miku.N requires access to the content of Gachapon bot messages and their attachments to provide its functionality.

By installing Miku.N in a Discord server, the person installing the bot acknowledges and agrees that Miku.N may:

* Read relevant messages generated by the Gachapon bot.
* Scan card images attached to Gachapon messages.
* Temporarily process those images using local OCR.
* Extract card information from the images.
* Use the extracted information to respond with the relevant card data.
* Perform wishlist matching and send related notifications.

Miku.N does not intentionally monitor general server conversations.

The message and attachment processing described above is limited to providing Miku.N's Gachapon-related functionality.

## 4. Data Storage

Miku.N does not permanently store Gachapon message content or extracted card text.

Card images may be temporarily stored on the bot's server while they are being processed and are deleted after processing.

Other information required for Miku.N's functionality, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, wishlist information, and bot settings, may be stored.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Miku.N uses locally hosted Tesseract OCR to process card images.

Card images are not sent to third-party AI or OCR services for processing.

## 6. User Responsibility

You are responsible for using Miku.N in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service and applicable rules.

Miku.N is provided as a utility service and may occasionally experience errors, downtime, or incorrect card recognition.

## 7. Removing the Bot

A server administrator can remove Miku.N from a server at any time.

Removing Miku.N prevents it from processing future Gachapon messages in that server.
